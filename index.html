Nepal Civil War: who called them terrorists?

During the civil war (1996-2006) between Maoist insurgents and the Nepal government, the government labeled the Maoists "terrorists" in 2001. This label was removed in 2003, but reinforced later the same year. In 2005, the king assumed executive power and declared an emergency. We evaluate how one English weekly handled the government's directives to the media. The newspaper is Nepali Times; we use its print issues from 2001–2005.
